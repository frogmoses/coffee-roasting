# Review of 2026-09-18: does the system reach the goal?

The goal, in the operator's words: given a bean, develop a roasting profile
from his tastes and the system's knowledge, run the roast by Artisan alarms
(he loads, charges, tastes), and adjust from cupping. The second-order goal
that came out of the discussion: he should be able to say "let's work on
making it sweeter" and have the system pursue that through experiments, with
the cup as the judge.

Verdict on that day: every stage exists and is tested, no roast has run on an
exported alarm set, and the taste loop as built cannot pursue "sweeter". The
findings below are the change list. Two adversarial reviews (one on the
design-and-revise loop, one on roast day) produced the second and third
sections; the items marked *verified* were checked by hand against the
repository, the exported v2 rows, and Artisan 4.0.0's own source.

## A. The five changes held from the discussion

1. **Unbounded first design.** `design_profile` anchors v1 to the measured
   staircase with a six-change budget and the revision bounds. For a first
   design (or a `--fresh` flag) apply only the hard rules; the staircase
   stays as the no-model fallback.
2. **Split envelope from recipe.** The envelope welds machine facts (FC
   statistics, turning point, heat-soak, lag, ambient) to one recipe (the
   median staircase of the last six roasts). Hand the designer the machine
   facts as knowledge and the staircase as "one schedule that ran here".
3. **First-session bracket on a pre-FC lever.** The contrast lever already
   allows `first_cut_bt`; a new bean's first session should be able to
   bracket early heat instead of development.
4. **A goal channel.** `profile --revise --goal "sweeter"`, stored on the
   profile and carried forward until cleared. The verdict says what happened;
   the goal says which way to move. The flavor hypothesis and the contrast
   lever are written against it; the intake's sweetness and preference
   answers score it.
5. **Composite changes.** Let a revision say "shift every heater cut by
   -15F" or "stretch DRY to FC by 45 s" and count it as one bounded change,
   so the reviser can make a move the machine can register (a lone 10% step
   cannot be felt; the three-change budget cannot move a four-cut ladder).

Also from the discussion, not yet in the list above: one voice proposing
changes (the per-roast recommender becomes diagnosis only; the reviser owns
proposals); rank the bracket instead of rating it; an experiment record
(goal, lever, arms, ranking, verdict) in one block; retire the median
staircase once beans roast on profiles (baseline = reference or latest
exported version, never a blend).

## B. The goal loop (design review), in addition to A

6. **Development history carries the raw FC mark.** *Verified.* Batch #21:
   `dev_phase_time` 116 s, curve offset +46 s, audio offset +49 s. Its
   development was about 70 s. `_dev_history` and `attach_roast` store only
   the raw figure, so v2's reasoning ("the sour batches were the short
   ones") rests on a wrong number. Fix: `dev_s_corrected` (dev minus the
   audio offset, else the curve offset) and `mark_suspect` in the dev
   history and the versions block.
7. **Sweetness is pre-wired to development.** `INTAKE_LEGEND` tells the
   model sweetness rises with development; development has 30 s of headroom
   left before the 180 s cap. Fix: remove the causal sentence; with the goal
   channel, enumerate candidate levers and let the model bracket any.
8. **Only a development bracket can be executed.** `batches_from_profile`
   expands only `dev_seconds`; one `.alrm` per version; no roast is linked to
   a bracket arm. Fix: export `profile-vN-armK.alrm` for any lever, store
   `arm` on the roast link, show arms as a table in the versions block. The
   deepest structural gap.
9. **No minimum bracket width.** v2's 130/150/170 s with a hand-timed DROP
   and a mark error of tens of seconds is inside the noise. Fix: the
   validator refuses a `contrast_lever` spread under max(2 x FC time SD,
   45 s) for `dev_seconds`, with per-lever minimums.
10. **Charge temperature is a phantom lever.** `charge.bt` and
    `charge.warmup.charge_bt` are never reconciled; the change is free in
    `diff_schedules`. Fix: set the warm-up's charge BT from `charge.bt` in
    the validator and count it as a change with a +/-20F bound.
11. **Maillard length cannot be controlled or observed.** `after: "DRY"`
    triggers are exported as CHARGE-clock rows using the median DRY time;
    `maillard_s` is not in the dev history or the FC stats; not a contrast
    lever. Fix: emit From DRY END rows; add `maillard_s = fc_time -
    dry_time`; add it to the lever enum.
12. **A borrowed envelope carries the Rwanda's taste history.**
    `borrow_envelope` keeps `dev_history`, `ambient`, and the cup fields;
    `_best_dev` picks the Rwanda's best-cupped development for the new bean.
    Fix: drop them in `borrow_envelope`; skip `_best_dev` when borrowed.
13. **`_best_dev` ignores sweetness and hard-codes "balanced is the goal".**
    Fix: score on preference and drink-again, or on the active goal axis;
    delete the sentence from the legend.
14. **Taste theory as hard validator errors.** Heater never rises, heater
    into FC >= 40, fan <= 60 are errors, not measurements. A gentle start
    with recovery for a natural is refused. Fix: one pre-DRY rise <= 20% is
    a warning; `MIN_LAST_HEATER` and `MAX_FAN` move into per-bean prefs.
15. **The intake is weaker than the loop assumes.** "Versus previous batch"
    does not say cupping or roasting order; no arm or ranking question; all
    three cupped Rwanda roasts were brewed as cupping bowls, not with the
    Switch; batch position and ambient are not joined to the verdict. Fix:
    `arm` and `rank_on_goal` questions; store `batch_index_in_day` and
    `ambient_temp` with the verdict.
16. **The execution summary reports seconds only.** `compare_execution`
    computes `delta_f` but the summary line shows only time deltas, so a
    clock-fallback roast reads as "on schedule". Fix: max `delta_f` in the
    summary.

## C. Roast day (alarm review), fix before the first live session

1. **The warm-up fires before CONTROL is pressed; the drum never heats.**
   *Verified in Artisan source.* Rows 2 and 3 fire at ON + 1 s and + 3 s;
   `hottop.py setHottop` stores nothing unless `_control_active`; the sheet
   has ON, then super-user, then CONTROL. Fix: a custom button with the
   Hottop command `control(1)` (`main.py`: `QTimer.singleShot(1,
   HottopControlOn)` with `autosuper=True`, so it also sets super-user;
   `takeHottopControl` is idempotent), fired by row 2 ahead of Motor On; the
   manual super-user and CONTROL steps leave the sheet.
2. **A late CHARGE mark leaves the heater at 100% until 398F.** *Verified
   in the v2 rows.* Rows 12-15 are If-Alarm guarded by row 9 (`CHARGE BT <
   252F`), clock twins included; mark CHARGE after the rebound and nothing
   cuts. Fix: row 9 gets a clock twin at CHARGE + 45 s.
3. **OFF is not a panic button.** With recording off no From-CHARGE row
   evaluates (the 398F ceiling included) and the driver keeps sending the
   last heater value; the only stop left is Artisan's 428F cutoff. Fix: the
   sheet says DROP is the panic button, never OFF; if the timer is not
   running you have not pressed START.
4. **Skipping COOL END carries an open door and full fan into the next
   warm-up.** The warm-up rows restart only the motor. Fix: row 2 also
   closes the door and stops the stirrer; a fan 0% row at ON; the sheet says
   COOL END before OFF, always.
5. **Paired rows confound a bracket session.** A cold first batch runs on
   the clock twins, a heat-soaked third batch on the BT twins, so the pre-FC
   schedule differs with roast order. Both reviews found this. Fix: a
   bracket session exports in one mode (BT tracks the bean through the
   day's heat-soak); paired stays for single-batch days.
6. **The latency subtraction assumes the ear marks FCs, but the sheet tells
   the operator to mark first.** When his hand wins, development is 18 s
   short. Fix: one marker per session, stated on the sheet; a manual session
   uses latency 0.
7. **Sheet and setup contradictions.** "Wait for ET within 10F" versus rows
   7 and 8, which set heater 100% as soon as BT drops under 300F; the FC
   watch-for lines versus the manual FC step; the quantifier-off setting not
   confirmed on the roaster (`deploy.sh diff` must be clean before Artisan
   starts); "save before ON" because the ear's rollover waits on the save;
   the documented capture gain is the one that clipped every 2026-09-13
   recording (`level` check with a target peak as a sheet step).
8. **Ear gate, for the automatic phase.** `min_elapsed_s` 480 is under the
   measured earliest FC of 556 s; a late start pre-satisfies it; Artisan
   cannot un-mark a pushed FCs, so a false declaration drops the batch at
   about real first crack. Fix: raise the minimum, and never push from a
   provisional clock.

Confirmed sound by the same review: a row fires on either its offset or its
temperature; guards are 0-based and the roast marker wins ties; the alarm
state resets on every ON; a slider alarm always sends the Hottop command;
rows 12-15 cannot fire out of order; BT cannot jump to 398F between samples;
the 370F warm-up cut peaks about 12F higher, far under 408F.
